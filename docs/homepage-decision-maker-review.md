## What changed and why it helps a decision-maker

The homepage now answers, in order, the questions a managing director or operations lead asks: what do you do for me, prove it, which of my problems do you solve, who is accountable, how do you deliver, who else says so, how do I talk to you. The team block sits between the two text-heavy sections to break them up, and section backgrounds alternate so every section start is visible. The funding disclosure lives in the footer on every page; the certification status sits at the bottom of the About page, because certification is in progress and the team matters more.

Owner review on 14 September 2026 changed the first version in these ways, all applied: the client case block became a page-wide carousel with one slide per case and the quote carousel is back (both fed from `work.ts`), the "defined first step" section is removed so the homepage does not decide for the visitor, the "working in regulated environments" section is folded into "How we work" with the compliance statement following it, the "(title at the time of the project)" suffix and the one-client sentence are removed, company details moved from the contact section to the About page, and the contact section is reduced to a heading, one sentence, the booking action and one line.

Hero (`app/components/Hero.tsx`). One headline with the business outcome ("Higher throughput. Lower running costs."), chosen after the owner's discussion with a prospect: internal capacity is not a purchase trigger for a buyer who pays both his own team and a consultant, whereas throughput, onboarding speed and running costs are, and all three are evidenced in the published cases (order setup time down 75%, delivery from 1.5 days to 3 hours, onboarding from months to under two weeks, IT running costs down 37%). One sentence saying what we do (custom software and system integrations), for whom and where (Europe and North America, as stated on /team/), two actions: book a free consultation (straight to the booking page) and see our work. AI is out of the headline; the AI page stays linked from the services section. On a 390 px phone the primary action sits on the first screen even with the cookie banner open. The hero fills the first screen (`min-h-[100svh]`): headline up to 96 px on wide screens, the sentence and the two buttons scaled to match, the marquee pinned to the foot of the screen. Behind it a slowly drifting mesh of the brand colours (`MeshGradient` in `app/components/ui/section-backdrop.tsx`) and a band of small sparkles rising from the marquee edge towards the headline, after ui-layouts' sparkles component, built on tsparticles (`@tsparticles/react`, `@tsparticles/slim`, `@tsparticles/engine` 4.4.0, new dependencies; `app/components/ui/sparkles.tsx`). Decoration only: hidden from assistive technology, still under reduced motion, rendered on the client (the headline, sentence, buttons and marquee label are in the static HTML).

Logo marquee (`app/components/LogoMarquee.tsx`, `app/components/ui/marquee.tsx`, `app/data/partners.ts`). A narrow band directly under the hero with the customer and partner logos scrolling right to left, each linking to the organisation, pausing on hover, still under reduced motion, grey with colour on hover in the light theme and light silhouettes in the dark theme. Later the same day the owner asked to hide Lifespin and Onyx Biotech from the marquee and the "Teams we've worked with" grid on /work temporarily: `HIDDEN_FROM_MARQUEE` in `LogoMarquee.tsx` and `SHOW_CUSTOMERS_GRID = false` in `app/work/page.tsx`; both are one-line switches. Partners: the three memberships from the About page plus Veractis.io (mark redrawn from a screenshot, name typeset in the site font) and helix.tech.bio (lettering typeset in the site font), both added at the owner's request as organisations under the same leadership as Krafthub. Contact (`app/components/Contact.tsx`). Design. After a first pass that the owner found crowded, nested boxes were removed in favour of whitespace, hairline rules and shorter copy, and two whole sections were dropped. ## Defects fixed

1. `/about/` returned 404 while still indexed. `app/about/page.tsx` renders `LegacyRedirect` to `/team/`, noindex, canonical `/team/`, not in the sitemap.
2. Animated counters put start values ("0%", "Under 0 weeks") in the HTML. `AnimatedCounter` now renders the final value, animates only after mount and in view, and not at all under `prefers-reduced-motion`. The homepage no longer uses it (figures are plain text); the component stays correct for other uses.
3. Legal notice lacked registration number, register, postal code, representatives and a data protection contact. Added legal form, registration number 50203351951, Register of Enterprises of the Republic of Latvia, registered office Lastādijas iela 12 k-3, Riga, LV-1050 (address confirmed by the owner on 14 September 2026; postal code from the register), VAT number; date updated. Representatives and data protection contact remain owner items.
4. Testimonials existed in two places with drifting attributions. Single source in `work.ts`. The "(title at the time of the project)" suffix was added and then removed at the owner's request; the OWNER note on Reynald Vidili's title stays.
5. Found during validation, not in the brief: `ConsentProvider` replaced all page content with an empty placeholder until it had read the consent cookie on the client, so every exported page had an empty `<body>` in the static HTML (nothing for crawlers that do not run JavaScript, reader mode, link previews, or the meta-refresh fallback on redirect pages). Children now render on the server; only the cookie banner waits for the stored consent. This exposed a second defect: `contact-confirmation` uses `useSearchParams()` without a Suspense boundary and could not prerender; wrapped.
6. `border-border/40` and similar opacity modifiers on `var()` colours are not generated by Tailwind 3 and silently fell back to the preflight grey. New `--divider` token for hairline rules.
7. `darkMode: ['class', 'class']` in `tailwind.config.ts` made Tailwind emit the selector `:is(class *)` for every `dark:` utility, so none had ever applied; the dark theme worked only through the CSS variables in `globals.css`. Now `['class', '.dark']`; the few existing `dark:` utilities (mobile menu overlay and shadow, a badge on the assessment page) apply as intended.
