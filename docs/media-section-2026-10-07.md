# Media section: photographs and videos from the 2026 conferences and delegations

Date: 7 October 2026
Author: Claude (Cowork), at Alex's request. Draft until reviewed by a human; nothing here is deployed until the owner commits and merges it.

## What was asked and what was decided

The owner asked for a section that builds credibility and trust by showing pictures and videos of the events the company has taken part in. Four decisions were taken with the owner before any code was written:

1. Placement: a compact section on the homepage, between the client quotes and the invitation to talk, plus the full galleries on the existing /events page. The homepage section answers "where have you been seen" after "who else says so" and before "how do I talk to you", extending the section order recorded in `docs/homepage-decision-maker-review.md`.
2. Video: the recap video the owner attached (227 MB, portrait 1080 by 1920, 2 minutes 28 seconds) cannot go into the repository, because GitHub refuses files over 100 MB. The owner chose YouTube embeds and sent three LIAA videos. They are embedded behind a local poster (see below), so YouTube receives no request until a visitor presses play.
3. Captions name the organisation, the place and the date only. No individual is named, including public officials, so no per-person consent is needed. Our own people are the exception: the alternative text names Aleksandrs Gusevs where he is the subject of the picture, as he already is on /team/.
4. Photo rights: the owner stated that the delegation organisers (LIAA) shared the photographs for participants' use. The credit line is "Photos: LIAA" on the Berlin gallery and "Photos and videos: LIAA" on the US one, matching the "LIAA" credit used by the press coverage.

## What was built

Rotation (owner request, 7 October 2026, in place of a fixed six): the homepage mosaic keeps six tiles but rotates through every photograph flagged `homepage: true` (sixteen at the time of writing: two from Berlin, seven from the US, seven from Rostock). `getHomepagePhotos()` takes the flagged pictures in turn from each event, newest event first, so the six in the server-rendered HTML are already a mix. `app/components/PhotoMosaic.tsx` (client) swaps one tile every four seconds, in turn, to the next picture not on screen, cross-fading over the picture it replaces once the new one has loaded; it pauses while the pointer or focus is on it, while the lightbox is open, while it is off screen or the tab is hidden, and does not run under reduced motion. A click opens the picture on screen at that moment, and the lightbox then moves through the whole pool. `app/components/Lightbox.tsx` is the dialog extracted from the gallery so both the grid and the mosaic use it.

`app/data/events.ts`: new `EventPhoto` and `EventVideo` types, optional `photos`, `videos` and `photoCredit` on an event, `thumbSize()`, and three selectors (`getEventsWithMedia`, `getHomepagePhotos`, `getHomepageVideo`). The two delegations are added as past events (newest first), with five and thirteen photographs and, for the US mission, the three videos. Six photographs carry `homepage: true`. Everything the homepage shows is read from this file, so it cannot show something the events page does not.

`app/components/PhotoGallery.tsx` (client): a grid of 3:2 thumbnails, each a button opening the full picture in the lightbox (`app/components/Lightbox.tsx`, a native `<dialog>`: Escape closes, focus stays inside and returns to the thumbnail that opened it, left and right arrows move between pictures, a click on the dark surround closes, the page does not scroll behind it); hover zoom is off under reduced motion. The thumbnails are in the server-rendered HTML. The dark surface is on the dialog box itself rather than on `::backdrop`, which headless Chromium did not paint reliably.

`app/components/VideoEmbed.tsx` (client): a poster image with a play button and a "Watch on YouTube" link. Play replaces the poster with the `youtube-nocookie.com` player (privacy-enhanced mode, autoplay, no related videos from other channels). The player takes the shape given by `aspect`; a portrait video is capped at 24 rem wide and centred on narrow screens so it never towers over the page. Verified: zero requests to youtube.com or ytimg.com before play; the iframe source after play is `https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/<id>?autoplay=1&rel=0`.

`app/components/Media.tsx`: the homepage section, heading "In the room with the industry.", one sentence naming the conference and the organisations the pictures show, the rotating six-tile mosaic (two by three on desktop beside the portrait video, three columns on tablets, two on phones), the day-one video, a link to /events/ and a credit line naming LIAA and Photothek Media Lab GmbH (linked). Sized like the other sections (`lg:min-h-[100svh]`, content centred, dots backdrop).

`app/events/page.tsx`: event cards with media gain a "Photos and videos" action that jumps to a new "Photos and videos" section at the foot of the page, one block per event: videos first (three in a row on desktop), then the photo grid, then the credit line.

`app/sitemap.ts`: `app/data/events.ts` added to the homepage's source files, so the homepage `lastmod` follows changes to the galleries.

Images: `public/images/events/berlin-2026/`, `public/images/events/us-west-coast-2026/`, `public/images/events/rostock-2026/` and `public/images/events/hamburg-rostock-2025/`, 96 WebP files, 5.6 MB in total. Each photograph is stored at a 1600 px long edge (or its original size when smaller, quality 78), as a 720 px thumbnail under `thumbs/` (quality 74) and as a 480 px thumbnail under `thumbs/sm/` (quality 72); metadata, including any location data, is stripped. The site exports statically with `images.unoptimized`, so next/image cannot produce responsive variants; the thumbnails are therefore plain `<img>` elements with a hand-written `srcset` and `sizes`, and the lightbox offers the 720 px copy (the whole picture, not a crop) to small screens. Measured on the export (Chromium, scrolled to the section): a 390 px phone at 2x fetches 202 KB for the homepage section and 739 KB for all of /events, a 1440 px desktop at 1x the same, a 1440 px desktop at 2x 325 KB and 1.16 MB; before the variants and the quality trim the figures were 356 KB and 1.29 MB for everyone. Everything is lazy-loaded below the fold; full-size pictures load only when the lightbox opens; swapped-in mosaic tiles are fetched at low priority. The regeneration script is `regen_images.py` in the session's scratch space (28 originals in, three sizes out) and should be kept with the originals if they are archived. The three posters under `posters/` are 720 by 1280: the first second of the day-one video (Los Angeles skyline) and, as stand-ins, portrait crops of two 25 September photographs for day two and day three.

## Year and month filter on /events (7 October 2026, evening)

Owner request: buttons to filter the events by year and by month. `app/events/EventsList.tsx` (client) now renders the two columns under two rows of text options in the Work page's filter style (options on a hairline, an underline that slides to the selection): "Year: All years, 2025, 2026" (years from the data) and "Month: All months, January to December" with full month names. Months with no event in the chosen year are disabled, so no choice leads to an empty page by accident; changing the year keeps the month only if it still has events. The filter applies to both columns, a caption under it says what is shown ("2 events in September 2026. Show all"), and an active filter lifts the "earlier events" fold. The selection is mirrored in `?year=` and `?month=` (1 to 12) so a filtered view can be linked to; the static HTML always carries all twelve cards. Two follow-ups the same evening: the greyed months were not visibly grey, because `text-text-secondary/40` is silently dropped on this theme's CSS-variable colours (the trap noted in `tailwind.config.ts`), so they now use a plain `opacity-35` with a "Nothing in this period" title; and at the owner's request the control was pared down to its minimum: no "Year" and "Month" labels, no caption ("12 events. Choose a year or a month..."), one hairline under the months only, the sliding underline as the only accent, and on phones the months as one sideways-scrolling row instead of four wrapped lines. The count is read to screen readers only; "All years" and "All months" are the reset. An event that spans two months counts in both. The page passes the cards' data only (not the galleries' photo lists), so nothing is sent to the client twice. Verified in Chromium: 2025 shows the Hamburg mission alone; 2026 plus September shows the US and Berlin missions; "All years" with September keeps all three September events; "Show all" restores the four-plus-fold view and clears the URL; `/events/?year=2026&month=6` opens on HLTH Europe and the Rostock conference on a 390 px phone without horizontal scroll; no console errors.
